# Resources

Our lab develops and releases datasets, models, and tools to support research in Natural Language Processing, Information Retrieval, and Speech Processing, with a particular focus on Persian and low-resource languages.

---

## Datasets

### ParsVoice
**A Large-Scale Multi-Speaker Persian Speech Corpus for Text-to-Speech Synthesis**

The largest Persian speech corpus designed specifically for TTS applications, featuring 1,804 hours of high-quality speech from 470+ speakers.

- **Paper:** [arXiv:2510.10774](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.10774)
- **Dataset:** [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MohammadJRanjbar/ParsVoice)
- **Demo:** [ParsVoice Demo](https://mohammadjranjbar.github.io/ParsVoice/)
- **Year:** 2024

---

### PersianPunc
**A Corpus for Persian Punctuation Restoration**

A comprehensive dataset for Persian punctuation restoration, enabling models to accurately restore punctuation marks in unpunctuated Persian text.

- **Paper:** Accepted at SilkRoad NLP Workshop @ EACL 2025
- **Dataset:** [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MohammadJRanjbar/PersianPunc)
- **Year:** 2025

---

### PerCul
**A Story-Driven Cultural Evaluation of LLMs in Persian**

Dataset designed to assess cultural sensitivity of LLMs toward Persian culture through story-based, multiple-choice questions.

- **Paper:** [NAACL 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-long.702/)
- **Dataset:** [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/datasets/teias-ai/percul)
- **Year:** 2025

---

### PolitiSky24
**U.S. Political Bluesky Dataset with User Stance Labels**

First-of-its-kind dataset for stance detection on Bluesky, focused on the 2024 U.S. presidential election. Contains 16,044 user-target stance pairs with detailed metadata.

- **Paper:** [arXiv:2506.07606](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07606)
- **Dataset:** [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/14671773)
- **Year:** 2025

---

### PersianMedQA
**Language-Centric Evaluation of LLMs in the Persian Medical Domain**

Comprehensive evaluation dataset for assessing LLM performance in Persian medical domain, with parallel English translations.

- **Paper:** [arXiv:2506.00250](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.00250)
- **Dataset:** [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MohammadJRanjbar/PersianMedQA)
- **Year:** 2025

---

### Persian Offensive Language Dataset
**Hate Speech and Offensive Language Detection**

Dataset of 38K Persian tweets containing hate and offensive language, manually annotated with bias measurement metrics.

- **Paper:** [Machine Learning Journal](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10994-023-06370-5)
- **Year:** 2024

---

## Models

### ARMAN
**Pre-trained Model for Persian Abstractive Summarization**

Transformer-based encoder-decoder model achieving state-of-the-art performance on Persian summarization tasks.

- **Paper:** [EMNLP 2021](https://aclanthology.org/2021.emnlp-main.741/)
- **Code:** [GitHub](https://github.com/alirezasalemi7/ARMAN)
- **Year:** 2021
