We study how people search for and understand text, in Persian and across languages. The lab is part of the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Tehran and is led by Associate Professor Azadeh Shakery.

Our work covers retrieval models, cross-language retrieval, Persian NLP and LLM evaluation. It appears at SIGIR, ECIR, CIKM, EMNLP, NAACL and LREC, and we release our datasets and models openly.
